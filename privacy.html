Privacy Policy for Audifiy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Short version: Your books, PDFs, EPUBs, bookmarks, and quotes stay on your device. Audifiy does not require an account and does not upload your library to our servers. We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements so Audifiy can remain free.

1. Local Documents & Storage Permissions

Audifiy reads documents you explicitly choose to import or sync (such as PDF, EPUB, DOCX, ODT, MOBI, FB2, RTF, HTML, and TXT files) using Android's document picker and Storage Access Framework. Imported documents, reading progress, bookmarks, quotes, and reader settings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to or collected by the developer.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Audifiy displays banner ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain device and usage information with Google and its advertising partners, including:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general location)
  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • Ad interaction data (impressions and taps)
  • App diagnostic and performance information

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at Google Partner Sites Policy and review the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Optional Online Features

  • Translation: If you explicitly tap Translate or enable Auto-Translate, only the specific text you choose to translate is sent to Google Translate to return the translated text.
  • Define & Web Search: If you select text and tap Define or Search, Audifiy opens your device's web browser with that query.

4. Text-to-Speech & Media Notifications

Audifiy uses your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech engine to read documents aloud and posts a local media notification for playback controls (Play/Pause/Next/Previous). Audio is processed by your device's configured TTS engine.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Audifiy does not maintain user accounts or cloud servers, all reading data is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual books inside the app or remove all local app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Audifiy.

6. Children's Privacy

Audifiy is a general-audience document reader and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new features are added. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at: epiphanyvoyager@gmail.com.
